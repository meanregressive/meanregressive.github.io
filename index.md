I am a development researcher with seven years’ experience conducting rigorous evaluations across sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, having worked previously on implementing development projects at scale. I am a Senior Research Analyst at the [International Food Policy Research Institute (IFPRI)](https://www.ifpri.org), where I work primarily on randomized controlled trials to evaluate policies and programs focused on poverty reduction, women's economic empowerment, and household resilience.

Before joining IFPRI, I was at [Equimundo](https://www.equimundo.org), where I worked on engaging men in care work and preventing violence against women and children. Prior to that, I managed youth employability programs at Quest Alliance in India.

I hold an M.A. in Development Economics from the Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies and a B.A. in English Literature from Jadavpur University. I've also completed graduate coursework in Mathematics at American University.

I have been leading on the adoption of agentic AI for research at IFPRI. You can find more on my [research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}), [AI and coding work]({{ '/coding/' | relative_url }}), and prior experience in my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).
