I am a development researcher with seven years' experience conducting rigorous evaluations across sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia, having worked previously on implementing development projects at scale. I am a Senior Research Analyst at the [International Food Policy Research Institute (IFPRI)](https://www.ifpri.org), where I work on randomized controlled trials of a national school meals program in Mozambique, cash transfer and nutrition-sensitive agriculture programs in Bangladesh, and an ultra-poor graduation program in Somalia, as well as administrative data covering 13 million households in Egypt's Takaful cash transfer program. Much of this work focuses on women's empowerment.

Before IFPRI, I was at [Equimundo](https://www.equimundo.org) (formerly Promundo-US), where I led a three-year effort to harmonize the International Men and Gender Equality Survey (IMAGES) across 33 countries and managed a landscape review of research on adolescent boys in sub-Saharan Africa. Earlier, I managed youth employability programs at Quest Alliance in Mumbai. I hold an M.A. in Development Economics from the Johns Hopkins School of Advanced International Studies and a B.A. in English Literature from Jadavpur University, and completed graduate coursework in Real Analysis at American University.

I also lead the adoption of agentic AI for research at IFPRI. You can find more on my [research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}), [AI and coding work]({{ '/coding/' | relative_url }}), and my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).
